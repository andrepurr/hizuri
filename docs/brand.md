# Brand

**Bottom line:** hizuri's brand is one idea, Fluctuation: a still field of dots that your voice bends, with colour born at the bending point. Every brand element — mark, wordmark, motion — is built from that idea and from the decisions below; nothing is added that cannot be traced back to them. Interface tokens are in [design.md](design.md).

## Decisions already made (2026-10-16)

- **Concept:** Fluctuation, variant B "Spectrum" — a near-black field, a warm core, spectrum colours spreading out to the blue field.
- **Mark:** the 7×7 dot field pulled toward a warm core (`packages/app/src/components/icons/hizuri-mark.tsx`).
- **Name:** hizuri, always lowercase.
- **Type:** Geist and Geist Mono (SIL OFL 1.1). The wordmark is geometric monoline with circular arcs, in Geist's proportions.

## Wordmark spec

The brief for the "hizuri" wordmark. A variant that breaks any line is rejected. The idea in one sentence: the whole word is made of one element, the h, and its divisions — the i's split off from it, the dotted z divides it, the u and the r are the same figure again.

1. **h, u and r are the same figure.** The h is written like an "n" — no tall stem — with a small circle on top, exactly the i's dot. The u is that arch turned upside down. The r is the same shape without the dot and not drawn to the end. The geometry is identical, not similar: laid over each other, the shapes match.
2. **The two i's are what is left of the h.** All dots in the word are one and the same dot: the i's dots and the h's dot.
3. **The z is the separate part** — the divider and link between "hi" and "uri" — drawn in dots, the same dots as the i's and as the mark's dot field (the AI's face).
4. **It belongs to the brand:** the Fluctuation concept and the type decisions above (Geist proportions, monoline arcs). It reads as part of the same family as the mark and the interface.
5. **Rounded, calm, premium.** Circular arcs; no stencil gaps, no angular cuts, no cartoon shapes.
6. **Nothing invented.** Every element comes from this spec or the concept: the h's dot and the dotted z are the only dots it adds; no other dots, no ornaments.
7. **Works everywhere:** reviewed in place — the app header and splash, the icon lockup, a website hero — on dark and light, at large sizes and at 16 px, before it is shown as a candidate.

What breaks it: angular or cartoonish shapes, an h/u/r that no longer read as one figure, and invented dots (such as a dot over the h).
